90-day roadmap

DAYS 1–30

Foundation

Build consistency before intensity.

  • Walk 20–30 minutes most days.
  • Strength train 2 days, building toward 3.
  • Set a steady sleep and meal routine.
  • Learn Python basics and track spending.
DAYS 31–60

Momentum

Make good habits feel normal.

  • 3 strength sessions + 2 walks weekly.
  • Plan meals and office outfits.
  • Practise Git, Linux and Jenkins basics.
  • Review budget and debt repayment.
DAYS 61–90

Identity

Show up even when motivation is low.

  • Maintain workouts and recovery.
  • Finish one practical automation project.
  • Update CV and practise interviews.
  • Review health, savings and progress.

Simple workout guide

Full-body strength · 2–3 days/week

Warm up 5 minutes. Start with 2 comfortable sets each: chair squats, incline push-ups, resistance-band/dumbbell rows, glute bridges and bird-dogs. Use good form and stop if you feel pain.

Other days: brisk walking, mobility or rest. Gradually work toward 150 minutes of moderate activity per week.

Food guide

Breakfast

Poha with peas and curd, oats with milk, eggs/toast or besan chilla.

Lunch

Roti/rice, dal/beans/paneer or other protein, vegetables and curd if tolerated.

Snack

Fruit, roasted chana, yogurt or a small portion of nuts.

Dinner

Dal, paneer, tofu, eggs, fish or chicken with vegetables and roti/rice.

General wellness guidance only. Adapt exercise and meals to your health needs. Seek professional advice if you have a medical condition, injury or concerning symptoms.
